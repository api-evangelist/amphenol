Artificial intelligence has fundamentally changed the physics inside the data center. Racks that once operated comfortably in the 5 to 20 kW range are now exceeding 30 kW, 60 kW, and even 100 kW per rack. At these densities, air cooling cannot remove heat efficiently enough to keep up with power demands.
