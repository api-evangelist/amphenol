# Amphenol Sensors

> Amphenol Sensors unites 25 sensing brands measuring pressure, temperature, gas, position, vibration and more across automotive, aerospace, medical, industrial and energy markets.

Amphenol Sensors is a family of independent sensing brands — including Telaire,
NovaSensor, Kaye, Thermometrics and Airmar — part of Amphenol Corporation. This
site maps measurement needs to the brands and products that serve them, and
forwards inquiries to the relevant business unit.
